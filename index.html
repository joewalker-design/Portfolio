Who am I?

I'm Joe Walker, a multimedia designer based in Lancaster, UK, specialising in graphic design and 3D visualisation. I create brand identities, print and digital graphics, and detailed 3D environments and product renders, each shaped around the client's goals.

Whether the project is a logo, a poster, a website or a full 3D scene, I bring a considered, detail-focused approach, balanced with a creative and personable touch.

I am clear and direct communicator. You will have consistent updates of the work in progress and any feedback received will be handled constructively. I like to work with people who know what they want and are able to articulate it clearly.

Please reach out to me through my contact section or through the inquiry form with details about your project.

AI Policy

I use AI tools to assist my workflow, mainly to explore early ideas and research into various subjects. It will never replace my creative thinking and my approach to design.

We can discuss the use of AI for your projects and I’m happy to oblige a strictly no AI policy.
