About

First Class Honours graduate (Digital Design, BSc) — awarded best Final Year Project. Alongside completing my degree, I spent 2+ years as a Graphic Designer producing high-precision biometric systems visual work, where accuracy was paramount.

I love applying my brand identity experience to help growing businesses stand out with design that connects with people.
