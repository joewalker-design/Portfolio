Who am I?

Hi, my name is Joe, I'm a multimedia designer from Lancaster, UK; specialising in Graphic Design and 3D visualization. I studied at University of Kent, achieving a First-class Honours in BSc Digital Design, including an award-winning final year project ().

Whether it’s a logo, poster, website or a full 3D scene, I approach with a delicate balance of seriousness and playfulness.

What can I offer?

3D Design
3D production-ready models for product video showcases, including texturing, lighting, animations and simulations.

Graphic Design
Unique and memorable logos; elements from those logos are used to create full visual identities, extending into other areas of design for print and digital: Posters, Leaflets, Social Media and Web.
